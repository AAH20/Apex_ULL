## 1. Executive Summary

### Investment Thesis

Ultra-low-latency (ULL) infrastructure is the foundational technology layer for financial markets, payments, and real-time gaming. As trading volumes grow, payment authorization demands sub-100ms SLAs, and cloud gaming targets <50ms latency, the market for ULL infrastructure is expanding beyond traditional HFT into adjacent verticals.

This financial model projects the financial performance of a ULL infrastructure platform over three years, with revenue driven by four verticals: **HFT/Trading**, **Payment Networks**, **Network Technologies**, and **Gaming**.

## 5. Pricing Models

### 5.1 Pricing Strategy Overview

We employ a **value-based pricing** model anchored to the economic value of latency reduction for each customer segment. Pricing is structured to capture 10–20% of the customer's latency-driven revenue uplift.
